# SaTScan Community of Practice

## 🌎 About the Community

The **SaTScan Community of Practice** is a collaborative space for U.S. public health departments and partners using, learning, or interested in using **SaTScan** for spatial and space-time cluster detection.

The goal of this community is to connect practitioners across jurisdictions so we can learn from one another, share practical experience, and improve how SaTScan methods are applied in public health practice.

Whether you are new to SaTScan or have been using it for years, this community is intended to provide a place to ask questions, compare approaches, and discuss challenges that arise when moving from statistical results to public health action.

---

## 🎯 Community Goals

This Community of Practice aims to support discussion and collaboration around topics such as:

### Learning SaTScan

- Getting started with SaTScan
- Understanding available statistical models and analysis options
- Developing reproducible workflows
- Learning from other public health jurisdictions using SaTScan

### Analysis Settings and Methods

- Selecting appropriate spatial and temporal scanning parameters
- Choosing maximum cluster sizes and other analysis settings
- Comparing prospective and retrospective analyses
- Discussing methodological improvements and emerging approaches
- Comparing settings and approaches across jurisdictions

### Interpreting Results

- Understanding statistically significant and non-significant clusters
- Evaluating cluster size, location, timing, and relative risk
- Distinguishing meaningful public health signals from statistical artifacts
- Discussing difficult or unusual results with other users

### Cluster Investigation

- Developing approaches for reviewing detected clusters
- Determining when a cluster warrants additional investigation
- Connecting SaTScan results with surveillance, epidemiologic, laboratory, environmental, or other data
- Sharing investigation workflows and decision criteria
- Discussing questions that arise during cluster investigations
- Sharing lessons learned from previous investigations

### Implementation in Public Health Practice

- Automating recurring analyses
- Integrating SaTScan into routine surveillance systems
- Managing data preparation and data quality issues
- Improving analysis workflows
- Communicating findings with epidemiologists, programs, leadership, and external partners

---

## 🤝 Who Should Participate?

The community is intended primarily for people working in or with **U.S. public health departments**, including:

- Epidemiologists
- Data analysts and data scientists
- Informaticians
- Surveillance staff
- GIS professionals
- Public health researchers
- Academic and technical partners supporting public health agencies

Experience with SaTScan is **not required**.

Questions from new users are encouraged, and experienced users are invited to share examples, lessons learned, methods, and approaches that may benefit other jurisdictions.

---

## 📚 Sharing Resources

Community members are encouraged to share resources that may be useful to other SaTScan users, including:

- Example workflows
- R, Python, or other code
- SaTScan parameter files
- Training materials
- Automation tools

Please ensure that any shared examples follow your organization's data governance and privacy requirements.

---

## 🔒 Data and Privacy

Please **do not share identifiable, confidential, or protected health information** in this repository. Participants are responsible for following their organization's data governance, privacy, security, and data-sharing requirements.
